Firmware is updated manually by putting each hand in bootloader mode and copying the appropriate left or right firmware file to it. Once you're on x4.1 or higher, Keybard can also update directly for you over USB (see [Update your firmware from Keybard](/keybard/update-firmware/)).

## Before you start

1. **Save your layout.** Installing resets your layout and settings to the defaults.
   - Open [next.keybard.svalboard.com](https://next.keybard.svalboard.com) in Chrome or Edge and choose **Connect Keyboard**. If Keybard shows your layout, choose **Export Layout** and keep the `.svil` file.
   - If Keybard shows **Update your Svalboard's firmware** and says the board runs Vial, open Vial instead and choose **File → Save current layout**. Write down your DPI, scrolling, automouse and layer colors too: a `.vil` doesn't hold them (see [What a .vil doesn't hold](/firmware/#what-a-vil-doesnt-hold)).
2. **Download two files** from the newest [Svalboard QMK release](https://github.com/svalboard/qmk/releases): `svalboard_sval_right_<release>.uf2` for the right half and `svalboard_sval_left_<release>.uf2` for the left half, for example `svalboard_sval_right_x4.2.uf2`. They work with every pointing module.

Each file is for its own half only, and tells that half which side it is. A left file on the right half, or the other way round, stops both halves typing until the right file goes on.

## Update the right half

Plug the computer into the right half's **U** port. Keep the split cable between the two **S** ports.

<CadFigure name="cad-cables-right-master" alt="Correct, marked with a green check: both halves from above. A cable runs from the right half's U port to the computer. The split cable arches between the two halves' S ports." caption="Updating the right half: its U port to the computer, the split cable between the S ports." />

1. Press the right half's **RESET** button twice, about a quarter of a second apart. Too fast and nothing happens. A drive named **RPI-RP2** opens on your computer.
2. Drag `svalboard_sval_right_<release>.uf2` onto the drive. The drive closes and the half restarts. This is normal.

The two halves may not work together yet. That's expected until both are done.

## Update the left half

Move the computer cable to the left half's **U** port. Keep the split cable between the two **S** ports.

<CadFigure name="cad-cables-left-master" alt="Correct, marked with a green check: both halves from above. A cable runs from the left half's U port to the computer. The split cable arches from the left half's S port, over that cable, to the right half's S port." caption="Updating the left half: its U port to the computer, the split cable between the S ports." />

1. Press the left half's **RESET** button twice, about a quarter of a second apart. A drive named **RPI-RP2** opens.
2. Drag `svalboard_sval_left_<release>.uf2` onto the drive. The drive closes and the half restarts.

## Put your layout back

1. Plug in the way you usually do (see [Connect the cables](/start/connect-cables/)). Both halves type.
2. Open [next.keybard.svalboard.com](https://next.keybard.svalboard.com) and choose **Connect Keyboard**.
3. Choose **Import Layout** and pick the `.svil` (or the `.vil` from Vial). Read the review, then apply it.
4. Coming from Vial: set DPI, scrolling, automouse and layer colors again, and check your mod-taps: Chordal Hold replaces Achordion. Then choose **Export Layout** and keep the new `.svil`. For everything else that changes from Vial, see [Moving from Vial](/firmware/#moving-from-vial).

**Settings → Firmware and backups** now shows the new release on both halves. From here on, updates install from Keybard.

## Check the cables

If no **RPI-RP2** drive opens, or a half doesn't show up, look at the cables first. The computer always goes in a **U** port, and the split cable always joins the two **S** ports. Check the **U** and **S** letters on the case: a magnetic cable is easy to put on the wrong port.

## If a half doesn't type

- **A half stays a drive named RPI-RP2**: drag its file onto that drive again.
- **Neither half types**: a half may have the other half's file. Install `svalboard_sval_right_<release>.uf2` on the right half and `svalboard_sval_left_<release>.uf2` on the left half again, as above.
- **A half blinks red, about once a second**: the install didn't finish. The red stops by itself after 30 seconds. Check the cables, then install both halves again.
