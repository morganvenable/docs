Each half has two ports side by side at the finger end, marked on the case:

- **U**: the USB port. It goes straight to your computer, and nowhere else.
- **S** (or **↔**): the split port. The split cable joins the **S** ports of the two halves.

Both halves have the same two ports in the same order. Seen from above, **S** is on the left and **U** is on the right, on both halves.

The Hardware page's port figure caption said the left half is a mirror image; it now says the ports are in the same order. */}

## Everyday use

Plug one half's **U** port into your computer. Connect the split cable between the two **S** ports. Either half can be the one you plug in; if you have a TrackPoint, plug in the TrackPoint half (see [Connect the cables](/build/#connect-the-cables) in the build guide).

## Installing firmware

Plug the half you're installing straight into your computer by its **U** port. Keep the split cable connected, as [Install x4.1 on both halves](/firmware/update/#install-x41-on-both-halves) says. Firmware never goes in through the **S** port.

A cable runs from the left half's U port straight to the computer, and the split cable stays connected between the S ports." caption="Installing on the left half: its U port straight to the computer." />

## Never connect U to the other half

A **U** port never connects to the other half. If one half's **U** port is cabled to the other half's **S** port, that half doesn't show up on your computer. Move that cable so the **U** port goes straight to the computer.

<CadFigure name="cad-cables-wrong-u-to-s" alt="Wrong, marked with a red X: both halves from above. The right half's U port goes to the computer, and a red cable runs from the left half's U port to the right half's S port, crossed out." caption="Wrong: the left half's U port cabled to the right half's S port." />

Some boards come with small magnetic cable adapters in their ports, so the cables on the two halves can look different, and a magnetic cable is easy to put on the wrong port. Check the **U** and **S** letters on the case.
